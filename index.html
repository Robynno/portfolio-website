About Me

Hi and Welcome! I am an undergraduate student at Brown University double majoring in Computer Science and International and Public Affairs. I am passionate about exploring the intersection between our society and technology.

Fun fact: my last name Jecrois means “I believe” in French, reflecting the conviction I hold in the impact that designing with empathy and intention holds. My background in both Computer Science and International and Public Affairs has taught me to listen first, ask intentionally, and prototype thoughtfully in the projects I take on.
